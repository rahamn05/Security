Help & Safety

🔒 Never share OTPs, passwords or payment details with recruiters or “support” accounts.
💸 Genuine employers do not ask for registration, training or processing fees.
🔎 Check job offers on the company’s official website and careers page.
🌐 Inspect email domains carefully for look-alike spellings.
🚨 In India, report cyber fraud at cybercrime.gov.in or call 1930.
